Nakuru trade expo

NAKURU TRADE EXPO 2025 marks the launch of one of Kenya’s most dynamic trade exhibitions, proudly taking place from 29th to 31st May 2025 at the Rift Valley Sports Club in Nakuru. As the first event of its kind in the region, it brings together top industry leaders, forward-thinking entrepreneurs, and innovators from across the country. Attendees will discover the latest in agriculture, finance, energy, construction, manufacturing, tools, technology, and other fast-evolving sectors. NAKURU TRADE EXPO 2025 is more than just an exhibition — it’s a high-impact platform for networking, business growth, and exploring cutting-edge solutions that will shape the future of enterprise in Kenya and beyond.

Kakamega Trade Expo

KAKAMEGA TRADE EXPO 2025 successfully ignited Western Kenya’s business scene with its inaugural edition, held from 27th to 29th March 2025 at Golf Hotel Kakamega. As one of the region’s most anticipated trade exhibitions, the event brought together a diverse mix of industry experts, entrepreneurs, and innovators from across the country. Attendees explored the latest advancements in agribusiness, finance, green energy, construction, tools, manufacturing, and technology, gaining firsthand insight into the products and ideas driving Kenya’s economic future. More than just an exhibition, KAKAMEGA TRADE EXPO 2025 proved to be a premier platform for networking, knowledge sharing, and unlocking new opportunities for business growth across Western Kenya and beyond.

Eldoret Business Expo

ELDORET BUSINESS EXPO 2025 is an exciting upcoming event poised to spotlight the economic heartbeat of the North Rift region. Scheduled for September 4th to 6th, 2025, the expo will take place at the TAC Centre Grounds, behind Zion Mall in Eldoret City. Set in the vibrant town of Eldoret, this landmark event promises to bring together business leaders, innovators, SMEs, manufacturers, and service providers from across Kenya and East Africa. Attendees will explore a wide range of sectors including agribusiness, finance, construction, technology, renewable energy, health, and education. The expo offers a powerful opportunity to network, showcase products, forge partnerships, and uncover real-world solutions that drive business growth and innovation. Whether you're a start-up, an expanding brand, or an established enterprise, ELDORET BUSINESS EXPO 2025 is the place to connect, learn, and elevate your business to new heights. .
